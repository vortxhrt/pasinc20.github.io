Made with AI

Nailed It edits your photograph using a third-party AI provider. When you tap Generate, the photo you chose — and the inspiration picture, if you selected one — are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to that provider, which returns the edited image. We do not store your photos. The app asks for your agreement before any of this happens, and you can decline and still browse the rest of the app.

Results are a simulation, not a promise. What a technician can achieve depends on your natural nails, the products used and their skill. Treat the image as something to show and discuss, not a guarantee.

Subscription

Nailed It gives you one free look so you can see it work on your own hand. After that, Nailed It Premium is £8.99 per week after a 3-day free trial, or £39.99 per year, for unlimited looks.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of that period. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. If you subscribe during a free trial, any unused portion of the trial is forfeited. Prices are for the United Kingdom and may vary by region.
